Who is Madscope

Kamau Hinds, known as Madscope, is a Jamaican recording and performing artist bringing island energy, lyrical precision and range to today's dancehall scene.

Raised in Windsor Castle, on the border of St. Mary and Portland, he started out as "Scope." The name grew into Madscope as his sharp, precise delivery became a sound of its own.
