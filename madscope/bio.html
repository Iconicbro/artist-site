The story

Kamau Hinds, known as Madscope, is an emerging Jamaican recording and performing artist bringing authentic island energy, lyrical precision and versatility to today's dancehall scene.

Raised in Windsor Castle, on the border of St. Mary and Portland, Jamaica, Madscope draws inspiration from the culture, sound and musical traditions of his homeland. His name reflects his distinctive lyrical delivery. Originally known as "Scope," he evolved into "Madscope" as his sharp, precise style developed into a recognisable identity of its own.

Since releasing his debut single, Dream Big, in 2020, Madscope has kept developing his sound and building a growing catalogue. His releases move between energetic dancehall, party anthems, melodic records and relatable music.

His catalogue includes Way Life Set Up, Tipsy featuring Keyba Sparks and Andre De'Saint, and Wine Up, produced by Cornelius Daley in 2024. In 2025 he released Pull Up, a high-energy track made for DJs, dancehall sessions, parties and live audiences.

He has kept building momentum with Til a Mawnin' and Stack Paper, as well as Gal Fi Get, promoted on the Hill & Gully Riddim. His collaborations include Money Up with CD Bangings and Solid as a Rock.

Madscope is not just a studio artist. He has performed at numerous stage events and keeps developing his presence as a live entertainer, bringing different styles, energy and personality to each audience and room.

Managed by 1 Charge Productions, Madscope is focused on expanding his audience, strengthening his catalogue and creating opportunities locally and internationally.
